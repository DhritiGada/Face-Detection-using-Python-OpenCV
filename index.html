How it works

  1. Your browser opens the camera.
  2. A face-detection model runs on each video frame.
  3. Detected faces receive bounding boxes.
  4. The counter updates continuously.
Privacy by design

Camera frames are processed client-side for detection and are not intentionally uploaded or stored by this prototype.
