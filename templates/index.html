Use your camera to detect and count visible faces in real time. Detection runs through a Python/OpenCV backend.

Camera controls

Start your camera and allow browser access when prompted.

Detection pipeline

  1. The browser opens your camera.
  2. A frame is sampled several times per second.
  3. The frame is sent to the Flask backend.
  4. Python/OpenCV detects faces.
  5. The browser draws boxes and updates the count.
Prototype privacy

Frames are sent to the server only for detection. This prototype does not intentionally save uploaded frames or identify people.
